## Suggested purchase

For an initial interior, prioritize **Tech**. Add **Mega Pack II** if the slice needs more structures/objects; together they total €17.92 at reviewed prices. Nature can wait for exterior needs. Do not buy both texture packs solely for quantity.

[ALL IN ONE – The Humble Bundle](https://pizzadoggy.itch.io/the-humble-bundle) advertises all current/future packs for **€39** (advertised regular €150). The five items above total **€32.80**: the bundle is €6.20 more and makes more sense if other products are also wanted. Credit for a previous purchase was not confirmed. Future-pack inclusion is the seller's offer, not a guarantee about content or timing.

None of the reviewed listings confirms coverage for bodies/characters, a specific Brazilian reception, readable localized documents or a complete luxury set. These remain separate gaps. A shared author helps consistency, but palette, maintenance state and object selection still need review.

Before integrating a purchase, check its supplied terms and whether the existing purchase can be updated. No purchase, new-model download or game change was made during this research.
