# Evidence prose variations

Approved direction: vary wording of the same information to reduce repetition without generating new facts or changing probative strength. The GDD is unchanged.

## Content

28 models have alternatives: Amadeu's two encounters, fibres, garments, activities, timing, five objects, five use explanations, six conflict states, room requests and contextual assistance, cleaning, handover, checkout and repair records.

Each encounter with Vicente has four wordings; the other 26 covered models have three. There are **58 alternative bodies** in addition to base text. The initially ambiguous equipment record also has **two alternative introductions**. All have PT/EN translations. Short titles and IDs remain stable.

Sentences were authored in full, not generated by random word substitution. Information order, construction and rhythm may vary. Person, location, time, observations, testimony limits and document state must remain equivalent. A version must not make the witness more certain, suggest guilt, add events or omit the caveat that they left to attend elsewhere.

The five scene-trace families retain their existing 25 room/method physical descriptions. Their physical text did not receive alternatives in this stage. Two ungenerated accounts and legacy clinical models are also outside this batch.

## Authoring and contracts

`EvidenceDefinition.text_variants` lists alternatives to the original `text_key` in Resources under `data/catalog/definitions`. `initial_text_variants` lists introductory alternatives in the five records requiring clarification. The CSV contains localized text only; it does not duplicate rules, proof IDs or causal relations.

The session provides `prose_index`, calculated only from seed and observation ID. Presentation uses it to choose the base text or an alternative. It does not consult guilt, score, counterproof discovery or language, or consume simulation randomness. An observation retains its wording across reading, board, accusation and reopening with the same seed. Observations without an index (tools and older fixtures) use base text.

Schema 6 and generator 0.7.0 remain; content version becomes **hotel_prose_1**. Seeds still produce the same facts and arguments. Thresholds and difficulty were not changed.

To add alternatives:

1. Write complete body versions preserving all original facts and caveats.
2. Use exactly the base text's named parameters and provide PT/EN.
3. Register keys in the corresponding Resource without repeating the base or another alternative.
4. Run catalog, localization and variation tests; review meaning manually. Correct parameters alone do not guarantee semantic equivalence.

## Validation

`tests/prose_variation_tests.gd`: 8,360 checks across 100 seeds. Covers reproduction, PT/EN rendering, preserved person/location/time in testimony, no unresolved parameters, identical parameter sets across versions and no factual mutation. All four versions of each account were selected across the seed set.

Localization tests check every new key. Desk and conversation flows are checked in the rendered scene. Human review of speech rhythm during play remains necessary.
